Add to Cart Fly

A product image flies into the cart when you press Add to cart, and the cart count goes up when it lands. The product row never moves. Only a temporary copy of the image does.

Your task is to build it in script.js. Create the copy, place it exactly over the product image with getBoundingClientRect(), move it to the cart with gsap.to(), and remove it in onComplete. Then raise the count.

Read more about gsap.to() and getBoundingClientRect().
